Privacy Policy for BurKozel Online 3D

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game BurKozel Online 3D ("Application", "Game") developed by the independent developer. This document explains how information is handled within the game.

1. Information Collection and Use

BurKozel Online 3D does not directly require users to submit personally identifiable information (such as real name, home address, or phone number) to play. However, the game utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, ensure service stability, and provide gameplay features.

2. Third-Party Services

The game integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own Privacy Policy:

  • Google Play Games Services: Used for player authentication, leaderboards, and cloud save progression.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Used for processing secure transactions if in-game purchases are made (all financial data is handled entirely by Google Play).
  • Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase (if enabled): Used to monitor app stability, crash reports, and game performance metrics.

For more details on how Google processes data, please visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Log Data and Device Information

In case of an error or crash in the Application, data and information (through third-party SDKs) may be collected on your phone called Log Data. This may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device model, operating system version, app settings, and crash diagnostics.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided personal data, please contact us so appropriate measures can be taken.

5. Data Retention and Security

We value your trust in providing access to necessary game services. We do not store your private personal data on our own physical servers. Any gameplay data linked to your Google Play account is stored and protected according to Google's security standards.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users have the right to request the deletion of any data tied to their gameplay profile. To request data deletion, you may delete your saved data via your Google Play Games profile settings or contact us directly at the email address provided below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
