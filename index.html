1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Burkozel 3D Online (Буркозел 3D Онлайн) ("the Game", "the Application"), developed and distributed by MazKaidaProject ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed when you use our mobile application on Android and related services.

2. Entertainment Only — No Real Money Gambling

Important notice regarding in-game currency:
Burkozel 3D Online is a traditional multiplayer card game intended exclusively for amusement and casual entertainment. Virtual game chips, coins, ratings, and tokens earned or purchased within the Game hold no real-world monetary value and cannot under any circumstance be converted, exchanged, withdrawn, or cashed out for real currency, goods, or financial compensation.

3. Data We Collect and Process

To provide multiplayer features, cross-device profile synchronization, and ensure game integrity, we process only minimal, necessary information:

  • Authentication and Identity (Google Play Games Services & Firebase Authentication): When you sign in using Google Play Games, we receive an authenticated unique User Identifier (UID), your Player Gamer ID, public display name, and avatar initial. We use this exclusively to recognize your profile, save your game stats, and display your nickname on multiplayer tables.
  • Gameplay, Statistics, and Progress: We store game progress in Google Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database, including match results, win/loss counts, rating (MMR), virtual coin balances, friend list connections, and combination records (Bura, Moskva, Four Ends). In accordance with our Zero Client Trust architecture, trick validation and scoring are calculated securely server-side.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): All monetary transactions are handled entirely and securely through Google Play. We only receive cryptographic purchase tokens and order IDs to deliver virtual coins to your account. We never collect, receive, or store your credit card numbers, bank account information, or billing address.
  • Device & Crash Diagnostics: Anonymous technical metrics such as device model, Android OS version, and crash stack traces may be processed via Firebase Crashlytics to detect bugs, improve performance, and maintain app stability.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT track or collect precise GPS location.
  • We do NOT access your personal contacts, SMS messages, photo gallery, or microphone.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted enterprise platform services strictly to support the Game’s core functionality:

  • Google Play Services & Play Games Services: Identity, cloud achievements, account sign-in. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions): Authoritative server logic, database storage, and anti-cheat validation. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processing in-game microtransactions securely.

6. Data Security and Zero Client Trust

We employ strict security standards to safeguard your data. All communication between the Application and backend servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Client-side cheating is eliminated through server-authoritative logic where players never receive opponents’ hidden cards or arbitrary balance modification privileges.

7. Data Retention and Your Rights (Account Deletion)

We retain your gameplay data for as long as your account remains active. In compliance with Google Play Store policies and international data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account and all associated records.

To delete your account and all stored data, simply email us at mazkaidaproject@gmail.com with your in-game nickname or User ID. Your request will be processed within 7 business days, resulting in the permanent removal of your profile and records from our databases.

8. Children's Privacy

Burkozel 3D Online is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at mazkaidaproject@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete the data.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Effective Date".
