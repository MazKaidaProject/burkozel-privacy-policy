## 3. Data We Collect and Process

To provide multiplayer features, cross-device profile synchronization, and ensure game integrity, we process only minimal, necessary information:

- **Authentication and Identity (Google Play Games Services & Firebase Authentication):**  
  When you sign in using Google Play Games, we receive an authenticated unique User Identifier (UID), your Player Gamer ID, public display name, and avatar initial. We use this exclusively to recognize your profile, save your game stats, and display your nickname on multiplayer tables.
- **Gameplay, Statistics, and Progress:**  
  We store game progress in Google Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database, including match results, win/loss counts, rating (MMR), virtual coin balances, friend list connections, and combination records (Bura, Moskva, Four Ends). In accordance with our Zero Client Trust architecture, trick validation and scoring are calculated securely server-side.
- **In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing):**  
  All monetary transactions are handled entirely and securely through Google Play. We only receive cryptographic purchase tokens and order IDs to deliver virtual coins to your account. We **never** collect, receive, or store your credit card numbers, bank account information, or billing address.
- **Device & Diagnostics Data:**  
  Anonymous technical metrics such as device model, Android OS version, and crash stack traces may be processed via Firebase Crashlytics to detect bugs, improve performance, and maintain app stability.
